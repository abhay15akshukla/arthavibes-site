# ArthaVibes Terms of Use

Last updated: 2 October 2026

These terms apply when you use the ArthaVibes app for Android. By installing or using the app you agree to
them. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app. "We" means the developer of ArthaVibes; "you" means the
person using the app.

## 1. What ArthaVibes is

ArthaVibes is a personal income and expense tracker. It can read bank and payment SMS messages on your phone to
add transactions for you (in the version of the app that asks for SMS access). Everything runs on your phone:
the app has no internet access, no accounts and no servers. Our [Privacy Policy](https://arthavibes.in/privacy)
explains what happens to your information.

You must be 18 or older to use the app.

## 2. Not financial advice

ArthaVibes shows totals, charts, budgets, forecasts and reports based on what is in the app. They are
estimates to help you understand your own spending. They are not financial, tax, investment or legal advice.
Check important figures against your bank statements before relying on them.

## 3. SMS detection is automatic and can be wrong

The app recognizes transactions from the wording of bank messages. Banks change their messages, and some
messages are ambiguous, so the app can miss a transaction, read an amount or category wrongly, or count
something that is not a transaction. You can review, correct and delete any entry. You are responsible for
checking the entries you rely on.

## 4. Your data is on your phone

Your data is stored only on your phone, encrypted. We never have a copy, so we cannot recover it for you. If
you lose or reset your phone, uninstall the app or use **Delete all data**, your data is gone unless you saved
a backup file (Settings > Backup). Keeping backups safe is your responsibility.

## 5. Free and Premium

The app is free to use, with limits. On the free plan the app works with your **last 30 days** of
transactions: lists, charts and totals show those days, and transactions can be added or imported only for
those days. CSV export, backup and the review list always include all your transactions, on every plan. Older
transactions already in the app are kept, not deleted; they are shown again with Premium. The free plan also has limits on the number of budgets, recurring entries and custom
categories. The app's Premium screen shows the current limits.

**ArthaVibes Premium** removes the date limit and adds extra features, such as insights, PDF and Excel reports,
unlimited budgets and recurring entries, and extra colors and icons. The list in the app's Premium screen is
the current one.

We may change what the free plan includes in a later version of the app, for example the number of days.
Changes never delete your data, and they never reduce what a Premium period you have already paid for
includes (section 11).

Premium is offered as:

- **Monthly** or **Yearly** subscription, which **renews automatically** at the end of each period until you
  cancel.
- **Lifetime**, a one-time purchase that does not renew.

The price is the one Google Play shows before you confirm a purchase, in your local currency, including any
taxes Google Play adds.

## 7. When Premium ends

If a subscription ends, is refunded, or a payment cannot be collected after Google Play's retry period, the app
returns to the free plan. **Your data is not deleted.** Transactions older than the free plan's 30 days are
hidden, not removed, and come back if you subscribe again. Budgets and recurring entries beyond the free limits
are paused (kept, but not active) and come back too. Categories you already have stay; adding more than the
free number needs Premium. Premium colors and icons return to the default.

Restoring a backup replaces all data in the app with everything in the backup file, on every plan. On the free
plan, restored transactions older than 30 days are hidden like any other older transaction, not removed.

The app checks your Premium status with Google Play on your phone. Premium keeps working while you are
offline, for up to about a month for a subscription, until Google Play can confirm it again.
